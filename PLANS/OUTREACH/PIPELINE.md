# Outreach Pipeline (Local Network)

## Scope
- Region: **within ~45 minutes of PA Furnace, PA**
- Primary targets (next 4 weeks): **artists / DJs in Nukage’s genre lane**

## Objective
Build relationships that compound into:
- collabs (music + content)
- bill swaps / opener slots
- local scene credibility + referrals

## Weekly cadence (default)
- Weekly check-in: **Friday ~12:00 PM ET**
  - Note: skip/shift as needed (e.g., when Tom is unavailable).

## Standard weekly session agenda (30–45 min)
1) Review: replies + last week’s follow-ups
2) Pick 3 targets this week
3) Draft outreach messages (DM/email)
4) Set follow-up dates + next actions
5) Tie in current creative mission (Performance Template sprint) with 1–2 clips or a short “what I’m building” line

## Rules of engagement
- Keep it human. 3 targets/week beats 30 spammy DMs.
- Default message length: 4–8 lines.
- Ask for one concrete next step ("want to swap 1 track?", "want to do a bill swap?", "want to stream a set?"), not vague networking.
- Always log the last touch + next follow-up.

## Negotiation guardrails (draft)
- No unpaid “exposure-only” gigs unless it clearly unlocks a relationship or a high-signal opportunity.
- Prefer deals that trade value symmetrically: bill swap, collab, cross-post.
- Keep asks low-friction: a 10–15 min call, a track swap, or a short stream session.

## Status definitions
- TODO: not contacted
- CONTACTED: sent first message
- REPLIED: got reply
- FOLLOW_UP: waiting; has next follow-up date
- ACTIVE: ongoing conversation / collab in progress
- BOOKED: date locked
- CLOSED: not a fit / no response after N touches
