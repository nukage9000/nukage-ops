# Sprint → Lead Magnet → Subscriber Growth (Virtuous Cycle)

## Concept
Design creative sprints so they naturally produce a **lead magnet** that grows an owned audience (email/SMS).

Instead of posting "into the void," each sprint has:
- a clear creative mission (what Tom is building)
- a lightweight behind-the-scenes content stream (optional)
- a *specific deliverable* that can be offered to subscribers

## Example (FAWM)
- Sprint: February Album Writing Month (FAWM)
- Intended lead magnet: "Follow along" → get the finished album emailed to subscribers
- Result / learning:
  - Album as a deliverable is compelling
  - But attempting to finish an album **and** keep up heavy social posting created too much load

## Key insight
This works best when the sprint:
- has a deliverable that is valuable but not crushingly large
- avoids rigid, high-pressure deadlines *unless everything else is simplified*
- uses **batching** and low-friction capture so production doesn't get derailed

## System template
For each sprint:
1) Sprint name + duration (1–4 weeks)
2) Promise (the story people can subscribe to)
3) Lead magnet deliverable (what subscribers get)
4) CTA + capture path (simple, repeatable)
5) Minimum viable posting plan (keep it sustainable)
6) Post-sprint follow-through (deliver the magnet + next sprint tease)

## Lead magnet ideas that fit Nukage (lighter than a full album)
- "Performance Template Pack" (mappings/presets/mini-project) once stable
- "Edit pack" (a few clips / loops / samples)
- "Behind-the-scenes breakdown" PDF + project notes
- "Early access" to one track + stems preview

## Guardrails
- Never let the lead magnet sabotage the creative mission.
- Prefer 'one CTA per week' during heavy build phases.
- Keep deliverables small enough to actually ship.
