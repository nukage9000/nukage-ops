What: State College Creator Collective

Where: Voodoo Brewery - 201 Elmwood St, State College, PA 16801

We noticed a gap in our local scene: we have incredible producers, songwriters, and artists, but we rarely get in the same room to actually build something together.



We're having a monthly, informal meetup at Voodoo Brewery called the State College Creator Collective. I want to pull together a group of creators to act as the core of a new network. This isn't a lecture or a seminar—it’s a peer-to-peer space for high-level collaboration and industry growth.

Why join us?
Peer-to-Peer Themes: Every month we’ll pick a theme for a roundtable discussion where we swap real-world experiences and hacks. Potential topics include:

Our Release Workflows: Comparing how we each handle the "day-of" logistics for a Spotify drop.

The Gear Swap: Discussing the hardware and software we’re currently using to solve problems in the studio and on stage.

Collaboration "Green Flags": Sharing tips on how to find the right partners and manage studio session dynamics.

Burnout & Creative Blocks: A candid look at how we stay productive when the "day job" or life gets in the way.

The "Sync" Hustle: Sharing what’s actually working (and what isn't) in the world of licensing.

Short-Form Strategy: What are we actually posting on Reels/TikTok that works?

The "Content" Workflow: How to capture high-quality video in the studio without killing the creative flow.

The Matchmaker Effect: We want to bridge the gap between skill sets—linking vocalists with producers, and artists with promoters.

The "Aux Exchange": Bring closed-back headphones and splitters. We’ll be swapping WIPs (Works in Progress) and getting honest feedback from a group of peers who actually understand the craft.

The Sync Pipeline: We’re partnering with a Spaces In Between Productions to help them source experienced local talent for upcoming placements.

We are specifically looking for people with skin in the game. We’ll eventually open this to beginners, but for this first phase, we’re focusing on the core pros.
