# Nukage Operating System (Canonical)

This document defines the canonical operating model for building Nukage in 2026+.

## Core principles
- **Momentum > rigidity**: protect wings; use gentle nudges to start; hands-off during flow.
- **Ship to learn**: minimum viable shipping beats perfect preparation.
- **Use systems to compound**: every week should leave behind reusable assets.
- **Pride over spaghetti**: social posting should feel like real art, not filler.

---

## The 3 Pillars (Big Three)

### 1) Live visuals (Moat Builder)
Goal: a signature performance aesthetic that’s hard to copy.
- Launchpad performance + audio effects
- Next phase: visuals triggered live (TouchDesigner / Blender / AI visuals)

### 2) Vocal flips / acapella bangers (Audience Builder)
Goal: turn your recorded vocals/acapellas into sticky hooks.
- Vocals increase shareability and memorability.

### 3) Marketing automation that discovers real fans (Engine)
Goal: stop posting into the void; build an owned audience.
- Convert attention → email/SMS list → retargetable demand.

---

## The Intelligence Layer (Opportunity Sniper)

Purpose: surface *a small number* of high-confidence trend opportunities that match Nukage.

Output format (per opportunity):
- Trend: sound/format/meme
- Why it’s hot
- Nukage fit (energy + sound identity)
- Action (what to make)
- Deadline (24–72h)
- Fan capture plan (email gate, “edit pack”, early access)

Three filters:
1) Time window (still rising)
2) Sound fit (Nukage lane)
3) Capture fit (drives owned audience)

Execution modes:
- Phase 1: manual capture (links/screenshots) → bot synthesis into briefs
- Phase 2: scheduled "trend sessions" with browser relay (10 min) → briefs

## Weekly cadence: Hero / Engine / Bridge

Each week should include:
- **Hero**: 1 high-art moment (visual-performance clip or flagship creative)
- **Engine**: 1 repeatable output (vocal flip hook, content batch, etc.)
- **Bridge**: 1 action that creates the next opportunity (collab DM, booking inquiry, list growth asset)

## Social system (Instagram-first)

Constraint: Tom is allergic to low-effort posting; frequency isn’t the goal.

Strategy:
- **Stories**: proof-of-work / vibe / progress on days you’re actually doing something.
- **Reels**: flagship moments only; intentional editing; strong hook.

Primary series direction:
- Performance-first clips built around **released tracks**.
- Use a Hero/Medium/Minimum ladder to keep identity while scaling effort.

Low-effort complement:
- ComfyUI batch generator (text overlay + released music clip) for hook testing.

## Live Looping Engine (Endlesss → Bitwig scenes → performance songs)

Context:
- Nukage’s music creation process is performance-native: composition and arrangement are built around live triggering.

2025 system (FAWM):
- Used **Endlesss** to record many loops live.
- Performed/recorded the looping sessions on **Twitch** and **YouTube**.
- Imported loops into **Bitwig** (and other tools as needed).
- Layered loops into genre-based "scenes" (drumstep, hardwave, and other EDM genres).
- Live-triggered scenes + live FX to create transitions between scenes.

Why it matters:
- This is a speed + authenticity advantage: songs are born from real performance, not only offline production.
- It produces content naturally (streamed sessions) and creates a reusable loop library.

2026 direction:
- Take the Live Looping Engine further (more refined scene library, smoother transitions, tighter performance template, and integration with visuals).

### 2026 upgrade: TouchOSC control layer inside Bitwig
- Built a **TouchOSC** template that replicates much of Endlesss’ looping workflow *directly in Bitwig*.
- TouchOSC acts as:
  - an **automation layer** for loop recording in Bitwig
  - a **MIDI translation layer** for controllers so gear behaves more intelligently/consistently
- Strategic benefit: reduces tool friction and keeps the workflow performance-native while staying in the DAW.
