# Evergreen Email Sequence — MMM-style (Narrative) — v1

See also: [`FUNNEL_FRAMEWORKS_SYNTHESIS.md`](./FUNNEL_FRAMEWORKS_SYNTHESIS.md) (recommended 4-touch nurture spine + funnel diagnostics).

Purpose: reusable follow-up sequence for Nukage campaigns (VIP/original/cover/flip). Friendly tone, narrative framing, single primary CTA.

Primary CTA: **Buy the album**
Secondary micro-CTA (allowed): **Reply to this email** (deliverability + signal)

---

## Variables (swap per campaign)
- `{asset_name}` — what they opted in for (VIP / private link / EP preview / etc.)
- `{asset_link}` — delivery link (SoundCloud private, download, etc.)
- `{bonus_link}` — optional bonus original / extra track
- `{album_link}` — album purchase link (Bandcamp/Shopify/etc.)
- `{spotify_link}` — Spotify profile/album
- `{reply_prompt}` — 1-line prompt; change per campaign
- `{moment}` — a timestamp or “listen for X” cue

---

## Email 0 — Delivery + micro-commitment (Day 0)
**Subject:** Your private link: {asset_name}
**Preheader:** Quick question after you listen.

Hey — thanks for grabbing this.

Here’s **{asset_name}**:
{asset_link}

Bonus (optional):
{bonus_link}

If you listen to one moment only: **{moment}**.

Then hit reply with one word: **{reply_prompt}**
(Example: “heavier” / “weirder” / “more melodic”)

If you want to support the whole project (and keep this pipeline alive):
**Buy the album** → {album_link}

— Tom / Nukage

---

## Email 1 — The “why” + what to expect (+1 day)
**Subject:** The real reason I’m building this list
**Preheader:** No spam. Just the good stuff.

Quick context: I’m Tom (Nukage).

I’m building a small-but-serious community around heavy bass music — the kind that feels like **metal + gaming adrenaline**, not generic EDM.

This list exists for one reason:
**to share the best work directly** without playing the algorithm game 24/7.

What you’ll get (when it’s worth your attention):
- private listens / VIPs
- the story behind releases (short, not blog posts)
- and the cleanest way to support when you actually want to

Here’s the link again if you need it:
{asset_link}

If you reply and tell me what you’re into (2 artists), I’ll tailor what I send next.

Support link (no pressure, just here):
**Buy the album** → {album_link}

---

## Email 2 — Origin story / mission beat (+3 days)
**Subject:** The moment I realized I needed a different plan
**Preheader:** (Short version.)

I used to think the job was “make music” and everything else would follow.

What I learned the hard way: the job is **make music + keep the signal** — otherwise great tracks just… evaporate.

So the plan now is simple:
- ship work consistently
- test what actually hits
- and build a real audience I can reach without begging a feed

That’s why you got **{asset_name}** first: it’s part of the “keep the signal” system.

If you want to be part of the momentum:
**Buy the album** → {album_link}

Reply question (it directly steers the next release):
Do you want the next thing to be **A)** heavier **B)** more melodic **C)** more chaotic?

---

## Email 3 — Proof / credibility (human, not braggy) (+5 days)
**Subject:** A quick way to “place” my sound
**Preheader:** If you liked {asset_name}, start here.

If **{asset_name}** landed for you, here are two better entry points so you don’t have to dig:

1) Best “starter” track: {spotify_link}
2) Second vibe / bonus: {bonus_link}

The goal isn’t to dump links — it’s to make it easy to find the *right* lane.

If you want to support the project in a way that actually matters:
**Buy the album** → {album_link}

And if you reply with what you’re working on (or what you listen to), I read it.

---

## Email 4 — Invitation + single ask (+7 days)
**Subject:** Want me to keep making this kind of music?
**Preheader:** Here’s the clean link.

At this point you’ve heard the sound. You get the vibe.

If you want more of it — and you want it to keep getting better — here’s the simplest way to back it:

**Buy the album** → {album_link}

If you’re not buying right now, still useful: hit reply with the one thing you want next:
- VIPs / originals / flips / covers?

Either answer helps me steer the next batch.

— Tom / Nukage

- Keep bodies short; remove any section that feels like filler.
